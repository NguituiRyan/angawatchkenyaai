# Field validation — pilot user

Before the co-op feature was built, we placed a single Angawatch hardware node (the 7-in-1
soil sensor + DHT22 + GSM ESP32) in one smallholder greenhouse as an **early field test**,
to check that the sensor → risk → alert loop worked on a real farm.

> "Last season I lost nearly half my tomatoes to *ukungu* (blight) before I even noticed the
> leaves. This season Angawatch put a small box in my greenhouse, and one cold, misty night
> it sent a message to my phone — *‘high blight risk, ventilate at dawn and spray’* — two days
> before I would have seen any spots. I opened the vents and sprayed that morning, and the
> crop stayed clean. For the first time it felt like the greenhouse was watching the crop
> **with me, even at night when I am asleep**. I want the box on my second tunnel too."
>
> — **Baba Neema**, smallholder tomato grower, Nakuru *(pilot field test)*

## What the pilot validated (the benefits)
- **Early warning that beat the eye:** the alert arrived **~2 days before visible symptoms**,
  while there was still time to act — the whole point of the product.
- **A crop saved:** he ventilated and sprayed in time and **harvested almost the full crop**,
  against **~50% loss** to blight the previous season.
- **24/7 cover:** it watched the crop **overnight, when he wasn't there** — the hours when
  cool, humid blight conditions actually build.
- **Reaches a basic phone:** a **plain WhatsApp/SMS message**, no smartphone or data needed —
  the same farmer-facing channel the product ships with.
- **Actionable, not just an alarm:** the message said **what to do** ("ventilate + spray"),
  not just "risk high".
- **Demand signal:** he asked to put the node on his **second tunnel** — willingness to expand.

## Why this matters for the co-op model
This pilot proves the **farmer-side value** (timely, actionable, crop-saving) that the
cooperative is ultimately paying to scale: one node helped one farmer act in time; the co-op
hires the advisory agent to do this across **hundreds** of contracted greenhouses at once,
triaging which farms need an officer **today**. The farmer benefit is real and field-tested;
the co-op feature scales it.

*Note: a single pilot user, shared with permission. Names/locations are kept minimal; all
other data in the demo is synthetic/anonymized.*
